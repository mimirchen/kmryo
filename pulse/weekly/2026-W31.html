Cost-effectiveness analysis of transcatheter aortic valve implantation for asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis across nine European countries
EHJ Open  ·  2026-07-30
Using a lifetime-horizon health-economic model built on EARLY TAVR trial inputs (901 patients with asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis randomized to early TAVI vs clinical surveillance, median follow-up 3.8 years), researchers assessed early TAVI's cost-effectiveness across nine European health systems (Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, UK). Early TAVI was economically dominant in all nine countries, with cost-effectiveness probability ranging from 97.32% (UK) to 99.94% (Belgium), lifetime savings of £1,788 (UK) to CHF15,802 (Switzerland) per patient, and QALY gains of 0.18 (Germany/UK) to 0.23 (Switzerland); early TAVI also cut disabling stroke by 0.04-0.05, non-disabling stroke by 0.03, and heart-failure hospitalization by 0.12-0.14 events per person. Published in European Heart Journal Open, the analysis lands as CMS weighs expanding US TAVR coverage to asymptomatic patients.

TAVI JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions · 2026-08-01
Hospital Volume and Failure to Rescue Post-TAVR: Insights From the STS/ACC TVT Registry
Using the STS/ACC TVT Registry, researchers analyzed 487,159 TAVR patients (median age 79, 57% male) across 808 US sites from 2017-2023, stratifying major complications and failure to rescue (30-day mortality after a major complication) by annualized hospital volume. Lower-volume sites had significantly higher 30-day mortality (adjusted OR 1.12, 95% CI 1.00-1.25, p=0.045) and major complications (adjusted OR 1.19, 95% CI 1.08-1.32, p=0.0006), but failure-to-rescue rates did not differ significantly by volume (adjusted OR 0.97 per 100 cases, 95% CI 0.93-1.01, p=0.11); the overall failure-to-rescue rate among complicated patients was 11%. The findings suggest the volume-mortality relationship in TAVR is driven mainly by higher complication rates at low-volume centers rather than differences in rescue capability.
LAA EuroIntervention · 2026-07-30
Left atrial appendage closure with the AnchorMan or Watchman devices: 12-month results of the SAFE-PROTECT randomised trial
SAFE-PROTECT, a multicenter, open-label non-inferiority RCT, randomized 216 patients with non-valvular AF at high stroke risk 1:1 to MicroPort's next-generation AnchorMan left atrial appendage occluder (n=108) or Watchman 2.5 (n=108). At 12 months the primary clinical-success endpoint (freedom from ischemic/hemorrhagic stroke, systemic embolism, or cardiovascular/unexplained death) was identical at 98.1% in both arms, meeting non-inferiority (p=0.0004), with comparable echo-defined LAA occlusion. AnchorMan showed significantly less peri-device leak >3mm (5.7% vs 15.6%, p=0.04), while all-cause mortality, stroke, major bleeding, and device-related complications were low and similar between groups.
IMAGING Businesswire / Pathway Labs · 2026-07-31
Pathway Labs Announces World's First FDA Clearance for Multicondition AI in Cardiology and Partnership with OpenEvidence
EchoNext, developed by Columbia/NewYork-Presbyterian cardiologist Pierre Elias and commercialized by Pathway Labs, received FDA clearance on June 23, 2026 as the world's first AI tool to screen for multiple forms of structural heart disease -- including severe aortic stenosis, significant mitral regurgitation, heart failure with reduced ejection fraction, and pulmonary hypertension -- from a routine 12-lead ECG. Trained on more than 700,000 paired ECG-echocardiogram cases across the NewYork-Presbyterian system and validated across 20+ hospitals and 500,000+ patients, EchoNext-assisted clinicians achieved 77.3% accuracy, 72.6% sensitivity, and 80.7% specificity versus 64.0%/61.1%/66.1% with routine care alone -- roughly doubling detection of structural heart disease. Pathway Labs simultaneously announced a partnership with OpenEvidence to bring EchoNext to the AI platform used by more than half of US clinicians.

TRICUSPID GlobeNewswire / TRiCares · 2026-07-31
TRiCares Announces Initiation of TRICURE US IDE Pivotal Study for Topaz Tricuspid Valve Replacement System
German device maker TRiCares announced that the first patient has been enrolled and treated in its TRICURE US IDE pivotal trial for the Topaz transcatheter tricuspid valve replacement (TTVR) system, performed at Piedmont Heart Institute in Atlanta by Dr. Pradeep Yadav. The prospective, global, multicenter, 1:1 randomized controlled trial -- running at up to 75 sites across the US, Canada, and Europe -- compares Topaz against the FDA-approved Edwards EVOQUE TTVR system in patients with severe-or-greater tricuspid regurgitation, marking the first actively enrolling head-to-head RCT of an investigational versus a commercially approved TTVR device. Topaz received FDA IDE approval for the pivotal trial in April 2026 and is also being studied in a parallel TRICURE European Pivotal Study.
LAA Boston Scientific / PRNewswire · 2026-08-01
Boston Scientific Announces Results for Second Quarter 2026
Boston Scientific reported Q2 2026 net sales of $5.442 billion (+7.5% reported, +7.0% organic) and adjusted EPS of $0.86 (vs $0.75 a year earlier), with Cardiovascular segment sales of $3.624 billion. The company disclosed that Watchman left atrial appendage closure sales grew just 4% in the quarter (US +3%, international +18%), attributing the slowdown to a shift toward pairing Watchman with concomitant cardiac procedures such as ablation rather than standalone implants; it expects US Watchman sales to decline mid-to-high single digits year-over-year in the second half, cut full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $3.28-$3.32 (from $3.34-$3.41) and organic growth guidance to 5%-6% (from 6.5%-8%), and announced a $500 million restructuring plan -- a notable slowdown signal for what has been structural heart's fastest-growing segment.

VDyne Announces First Patient Treated in TRIVITA US IDE Pivotal Study of the TriNova™ Transcatheter Tricuspid Valve Replacement System
VDyne announced that the first patient has been enrolled and treated in the TRIVITA US IDE pivotal trial of its TriNova transcatheter tricuspid valve replacement (TTVR) system, performed by Dr. Konstantinos Voudris at Allina Health Minneapolis Heart Institute. The prospective, global, multicenter, 1:1 randomized trial -- at up to 70 US and European sites -- compares TriNova head-to-head against the FDA-approved Edwards EVOQUE TTVR system in patients with severe-or-greater tricuspid regurgitation, marking the first actively enrolling head-to-head RCT of an investigational device against a commercially approved TTVR system.
MITRAL PR Newswire / Valgen Medtech · 2026-07-30
Valgen Medtech's DragonFly™ System Receives EU Approval for FMR Indication
Chinese device maker Valgen Medtech announced EU (CE mark) approval for its transfemoral edge-to-edge DragonFly™ mitral repair system in functional mitral regurgitation (FMR), extending its April 2025 approval for degenerative MR (DMR) and making it the first China-originated transcatheter mitral repair system approved in the EU for both indications. Trial data showed a 1-year composite primary effectiveness endpoint (freedom from all-cause death, mitral reintervention, and MR >2+) of 86.7%, device implantation and procedural success both at 97.4%, and 66.7% of patients treated with a single clip; 100% of patients reached NYHA class I/II by 30 days, sustained through 1-year follow-up. The expanded indication follows the 2025 ESC/EACTS guideline upgrade of TEER for selected ventricular FMR patients to a Class I recommendation.
TRICUSPID MassDevice / Jenscare Scientific · 2026-08-01
Jenscare Scientific's LuX-Valve Plus Receives EU MDR CE Certification for Transvenous Tricuspid Valve Replacement System
Chinese device maker Jenscare Scientific announced that its second-generation transvenous tricuspid valve replacement (TTVR) system, LuX-Valve Plus, has received CE certification under the EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR) for patients with severe tricuspid regurgitation who are not surgical candidates. Delivered transjugularly with multi-angle adjustable and steerable release, it becomes the world's second approved orthotopic transcatheter tricuspid valve replacement system; more than 1,000 patients across dozens of countries have received LuX-Valve implants to date. The CE mark opens the European market to LuX-Valve Plus, adding a new competitor to the TTVR space alongside Abbott's EVOQUE.

TRICUSPID Abbott · 2026-07-31
Abbott Initiates TREAT TR Study to Show Real-World Outcomes for TriClip in Repairing Leaky Tricuspid Heart Valve
Abbott announced the launch of TREAT TR, a post-market real-world study that will enroll roughly 1,000 patients at up to 100 sites across the US, Canada, and Europe to evaluate the safety and long-term performance of its TriClip transcatheter edge-to-edge repair (TEER) system for tricuspid regurgitation (TR). The first patient was enrolled at Hospital Clinic de Barcelona, led by Dr. Xavier Freixa. The study builds on prior real-world data from 3,035 patients across 278 US sites in the STS/ACC TVT Registry -- presented at New York Valves in June 2026 -- showing outcomes consistent with the TRILUMINATE Pivotal trial, with significant TR reduction and improved quality of life.
