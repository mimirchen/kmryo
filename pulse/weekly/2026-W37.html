Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement or Medical Treatment for Paradoxical Low-Flow, Low-Gradient Aortic Valve Stenosis: REBOOT-PARADOX
Circulation  ·  2026-09-09
REBOOT-PARADOX is the first multicenter, open-label randomized trial dedicated to comparing TAVR plus optimal medical therapy (OMT) versus OMT alone in symptomatic paradoxical low-flow, low-gradient severe aortic stenosis (aortic valve area ≤ 1.0 cm², mean gradient < 40 mm Hg, stroke volume index < 35 mL/m², ejection fraction ≥ 50%), presented as an ESC Congress 2026 Hot Line and published in Circulation. Slow recruitment forced early termination, with only 120 of a planned 783 patients randomized (80 TAVR, 40 OMT; median age 82, 53.3% women), and 19 OMT patients crossing over to TAVR for symptom progression. All-cause death, the primary endpoint, occurred in 29.0% with TAVR versus 37.1% with OMT (HR 0.83; 95% CI, 0.41-1.66; P=0.60), and 5-year mortality was 44.9% versus 56.6% (HR 0.76; 95% CI, 0.43-1.35; P=0.35) — neither reaching statistical significance. The underpowered trial leaves TAVR's mortality benefit in this subgroup unresolved, supporting an individualized strategy of close surveillance with timely TAVR upon clinical deterioration, and underscores the need for an adequately powered trial.

TAVI CMS · 2026-09-12
CMS Finalizes National Coverage Determination for TAVR, Expanding Medicare Coverage to Asymptomatic Severe Aortic Stenosis and Dropping the Two-Operator Mandate
On September 10, 2026, CMS finalized its updated National Coverage Determination (NCD) for TAVR after a nine-month review, removing Coverage with Evidence Development (CED) requirements for symptomatic severe aortic stenosis while extending Medicare coverage to asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis for the first time (still under CED registries), informed in part by EARLY TAVR trial data (15.2% vs 24.2% for the composite of death, stroke, or heart failure hospitalization). The policy also drops hospital procedural-volume thresholds and, for the first time, permits a single operator -- cardiologist or surgeon -- to perform TAVR without mandating both an interventional cardiologist and a cardiac surgeon, though hospitals must retain on-site cardiac surgery capability and the heart-team concept. The Society of Thoracic Surgeons voiced disappointment over the single-operator provision, while Edwards Lifesciences welcomed the update as expanding patient access, even as its shares dipped about 2.8% intraday on September 11 amid questions on adoption timelines. It is widely regarded as the most significant U.S. Medicare TAVR policy shift in over a decade.
TAVI FDA / Emboline · 2026-09-10
Emboline Receives U.S. FDA 510(k) Clearance for Emboliner Embolic Protection System
Emboline, Inc. announced on September 9, 2026 that the FDA has granted 510(k) clearance for its Emboliner Embolic Protection System, a double-wall cylindrical Nitinol mesh filter with a self-sealing access port that provides full-body (not just cerebral) embolic protection during transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) by capturing debris throughout the procedure. Clearance was supported by the prospective, multicenter, randomized PROTECT H2H pivotal IDE trial (>500 patients across the US, Germany and Brazil; mean age 79) comparing Emboliner with the previously cleared Sentinel Cerebral Protection System: the 30-day composite of death, stroke, or acute kidney injury occurred in 4.5% with Emboliner versus 5.0% with Sentinel (noninferiority P=.0004), 30-day stroke was 2.0% versus 2.1% — among the lowest reported in a pivotal TAVR embolic-protection trial — while Emboliner captured roughly three times more clinically relevant debris and achieved a higher technical success rate (96% vs 87%).
LAA EuroIntervention · 2026-09-11
Multimodality Imaging for Pre-, Intra-, and Postprocedural Guidance of Left Atrial Appendage Closure: A European Left Atrial Appendage Closure Club (ELAACC) Expert Consensus Statement
A European Left Atrial Appendage Closure Club (ELAACC) expert consensus statement, published September 7, 2026 in EuroIntervention, sets out structured multimodality imaging guidance across the full LAAC pathway: preprocedural cardiac CT combined with transesophageal echocardiography (TEE) for LAA morphology, ostial sizing, and device selection; intraprocedural TEE or intracardiac echocardiography (ICE) alongside fluoroscopy to guide deployment and assess sealing and complications; and postprocedural TEE or multidetector CT at 45-90 days to evaluate device stability, device-related thrombus, and peridevice leak. The statement aims to standardize nomenclature and imaging pathways to improve procedural safety and reproducibility across centres.
TAVI Cardiovascular Business · 2026-09-13
Heart Failure Prevalence, Medications, and Outcomes in Transcatheter Aortic Valve Interventions: A Real-World Analysis
A real-world analysis published in the Journal of Cardiovascular Medicine (August 2026) examined heart failure burden and medication use in more than 37,000 US patients who underwent transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) between 2017 and 2023: 70.4% already had a heart failure diagnosis at the time of TAVR, 56.9% had a heart failure diagnosis recorded within one year post-procedure, and 18.6% experienced an acute heart failure episode within that year. Despite the high burden of heart failure among TAVR patients, many were not receiving guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT) after the procedure, the study found. The authors call for dedicated clinical trials testing whether systematic post-TAVR heart failure medical management can reduce heart failure readmissions and mortality.
LAA Boston Scientific / SEC Filing · 2026-09-11
Boston Scientific Says Manufacturing and WATCHMAN Shipping Are Fully Restored, but Cyberattack Will Likely Cause It to Miss 2026 Guidance
In a September 8, 2026 SEC Form 8-K, Boston Scientific disclosed that the August 25 cyberattack is likely to make it miss the net sales growth and adjusted EPS guidance ranges it issued on July 29 (approximately 3-5% Q3 net sales growth, 5.5-6.5% full-year growth, and full-year adjusted EPS of $3.28-$3.32) -- the first time the incident has been deemed to have a material financial impact. Separately, the company's official update said manufacturing, order fulfillment, and shipping are now "fully restored," with products moving through its global distribution network at or above normal levels; supply of previously disrupted structural-heart and rhythm-management products, including the WATCHMAN left atrial appendage closure device, pacemakers, and stents, has normalized, and remote-monitoring activation for newly implanted devices has also been restored, though the full financial impact remains unknown. The episode underscores how exposed structural-heart device supply chains are to cybersecurity risk.
TAVI Cardiovascular Business · 2026-09-13
Medical Societies, Aging Advocates Share Early Reactions to New TAVR Policies
Following the September 10 release of CMS's final National Coverage Determination (NCD) for TAVR, the American College of Cardiology (ACC), Society of Thoracic Surgeons (STS), and Society for Cardiovascular Angiography and Interventions (SCAI) issued a joint statement on September 11 welcoming CMS's efforts to evolve coverage policy while cautioning that, "as TAVR expands into new patient populations, it is critical to preserve the quality framework that has contributed to its success," adding that participation in national registries, externally benchmarked quality programs, and rigorous outcomes reporting remain essential. Aging-focused patient advocacy groups, by contrast, broadly welcomed the update as expanding older patients' access to TAVR.
LAA MedTech Dive · 2026-09-08
Boston Scientific Begins to Restore Shipping After Cyberattack, With WATCHMAN and Cardiac Device Supply Among the Disruptions
Boston Scientific detected unauthorized activity and a network outage across certain internal IT systems on August 25, 2026, disclosed the incident on August 26, and engaged CrowdStrike and other third-party experts for investigation, containment, and recovery; the disruption hit order processing and shipping and affected supply and activation of cardiac rhythm management devices -- including the WATCHMAN left atrial appendage closure system, a core structural-heart product -- alongside pacemakers, ICDs, CRT devices, and insertable cardiac monitors. An August 30 update found no signs of unauthorized activity since August 25, with impact limited to specific on-premises systems (cloud systems were unaffected), and as of September 4 the company had begun restoring shipping for the majority of products from its major global distribution centers while working toward full capacity, a reminder of how exposed structural-heart device supply chains are to cyber incidents.
MITRAL InnovHeart · 2026-09-10
InnovHeart Announces First U.S. Saturn Transseptal TMVR System Implant, Reaching 15 Patients Globally
Italian device maker InnovHeart announced on September 3, 2026 the first U.S. implant of its Saturn Transseptal TMVR System, performed August 25 at NewYork-Presbyterian/Columbia University Irving Medical Center by principal investigator Torsten P. Vahl, MD, as part of the CASSINI-US Early Feasibility Study. The patient, an 86-year-old woman with severe (4+) functional mitral regurgitation, received the valve via a fully percutaneous transseptal approach into the native mitral position, bringing the global implant total to 15 patients. The CASSINI program is evaluating the technical feasibility, safety, and performance of the Saturn system in patients with moderate-to-severe or severe symptomatic mitral regurgitation who are candidates for transcatheter mitral valve replacement.
