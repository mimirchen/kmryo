TCT 2026 Opens in San Diego With 28 Late-Breaking Trials Spanning Coronary, Structural Heart, Peripheral and Renal Denervation Research
TCT 2026 / CRF  ·  2026-10-31
The Cardiovascular Research Foundation's TCT 2026 meeting opened October 31 at the San Diego Convention Center and runs through November 3, featuring 28 late-breaking studies across six sessions covering coronary, structural heart, peripheral vascular, renal denervation and antithrombotic research. Structural heart highlights on the late-breaking agenda include the PROGRESS trial of TAVR versus clinical surveillance for moderate aortic stenosis (presenting November 2), one-year CLASP IITR outcomes for Edwards' PASCAL tricuspid repair system, and early feasibility data from Abbott's CROWN-MV pivotal trial of the Cephea transcatheter mitral valve replacement system.

OTHER Abbott · 2026-10-30
Abbott Receives FDA Approval for First and Only Device in the U.S. to Treat Leaks After Heart Valve Replacement Without Additional Surgery
On October 28, 2026, the FDA approved Abbott's Amplatzer Paraclose PVL Occluder, making it the first and only transcatheter device cleared in the US to close paravalvular leak (PVL) around surgical aortic or mitral valve replacements, offering a minimally invasive alternative to repeat open-heart surgery. Delivered via a leg artery or vein, the device has been sold in Europe since 2020 as the Amplatzer Valvular Plug III. The approval was supported by the prospective, multicenter, single-arm PARADIGM study of roughly 200 patients; Abbott says 30-day PARADIGM outcomes will be presented at TCT 2026 in San Diego (October 31-November 3).
TRICUSPID Edwards Lifesciences / Healio · 2026-10-31
FDA Approves Edwards' PASCAL Precision for Tricuspid Repair and PASCAL Ace With Capture Clarity for Mitral TEER
On October 21, 2026, the FDA approved Edwards Lifesciences' PASCAL Precision system for transcatheter tricuspid edge-to-edge repair in patients with symptomatic severe tricuspid regurgitation despite optimal medical therapy who are intermediate or greater surgical risk, based on the CLASP IITR pivotal trial (1-year data due at TCT 2026). The same day, the FDA cleared PASCAL Ace with Capture Clarity for degenerative mitral M-TEER, the first US-approved TEER system with fluoroscopic indicators confirming leaflet grasping during implantation. The approvals make Edwards the only US company offering a full repair-and-replacement portfolio across both the mitral and tricuspid valves.
MITRAL Abbott · 2026-10-31
Abbott Initiates Pivotal CROWN-MV Trial for Cephea Transcatheter Mitral Valve Replacement System
Abbott announced on October 29, 2026 that it has initiated the CROWN-MV pivotal trial for its investigational Cephea transcatheter mitral valve replacement system, planning to enroll up to 585 patients across the US, Canada and Europe with moderate-to-severe or severe mitral regurgitation or mitral annular calcification who are not candidates for conventional surgery or transcatheter repair. The first patient was enrolled at Ascension Via Christi Hospital in Wichita, Kansas, under Dr. Bassem Chehab. Delivered transvenously, Cephea rounds out Abbott's mitral portfolio alongside MitraClip (repair) and Tendyne (replacement); early feasibility data from the third-generation delivery system are expected at TCT 2026.
TAVI GlobeNewswire / Anteris Technologies · 2026-10-28
Anteris Announces Upcoming Data Release for Medium-Size DurAVR THV from EMBARK Study at TCT 2026
On October 27, 2026, Anteris Technologies announced that Dr. Azeem Latib will present the first 30-day clinical and hemodynamic outcomes for the medium-size DurAVR biomimetic balloon-expandable transcatheter aortic valve from the EMBARK study, in the Innovation Theater at TCT 2026 (San Diego, Oct 31-Nov 3). Since 2021, more than 130 patients have been treated with DurAVR across valve sizes in the EMBARK study and a US Early Feasibility Study; the single-leaflet biomimetic design aims to restore more native-like laminar flow, and the data will support the ongoing roughly 1,000-patient PARADIGM global pivotal randomized trial now enrolling in the US and Europe.
OTHER PR Newswire / GrOwnValve · 2026-10-28
GrOwnValve Achieves Primary Safety Endpoint in First-in-Human Heart Valve Study
On October 26, 2026, Germany's GrOwnValve announced that its first-in-human feasibility study (GECT-DZHK28, NCT05809856) of an autologous, pericardium-derived, growable transcatheter pulmonary heart valve met its primary safety endpoint: none of the first five patients, who completed six-month follow-up at Charite - Universitatsmedizin Berlin, experienced device-related death, major bleeding, reoperation, or acute device-related complications. The trial plans to enroll seven young adults with congenital pulmonary valve disease, and the first case was published in European Heart Journal - Case Reports. With only five patients and six months of follow-up, this is an early safety signal rather than evidence of the valve's growth potential or long-term durability.
