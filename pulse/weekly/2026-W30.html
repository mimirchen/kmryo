Twenty-Year Follow-Up After Patent Foramen Ovale Closure in Patients With Paradoxical Embolism
JAMA Cardiology  ·  2026-07-21
This single-center cohort followed 130 patients who underwent transcatheter PFO closure between 2001 and 2006 for a PFO-associated stroke (n=91), TIA (n=45), or peripheral embolism (n=1) for up to 20 years — among the longest follow-up periods reported for PFO closure. Twenty patients (15.3%) died (12.2% from non-cardiovascular causes), while recurrent ischemic events were rare: just 1 stroke (0.04 per 100 patient-years) and 6 TIAs (0.25 per 100 patient-years), confirming durable very-long-term safety and efficacy of PFO closure for paradoxical embolism.

TAVI JAMA Cardiology · 2026-07-22
Seven-Year Valve Durability With Transcatheter or Surgical Aortic Valve Replacement: An Ad Hoc Analysis of the PARTNER 3 Randomized Clinical Trial
An ad hoc 7-year durability analysis of the low-risk PARTNER 3 RCT (1,000 patients — 495 TAVR, 453 SAVR — across 71 US/Canada centers, last follow-up March 2025) found comparable bioprosthetic valve failure (6.9% TAVR vs 7.3% SAVR) and aortic valve reintervention rates (6.0% vs 6.7%) between arms. Thrombosis-related valve dysfunction was more common with TAVR (5.2% vs 0.9%) but was mostly early-onset, reversible, and rarely progressed to failure — supporting comparable long-term durability of the balloon-expandable SAPIEN 3 valve versus surgery for lifetime management in low-risk patients.
TAVI JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions · 2026-07-21
First Large Comprehensive Core-Laboratory Evaluation of Implantation Depth and Clinical Outcomes in TAVR: Final Global Results From the Optimize PRO Prospective Study
The prospective Optimize PRO study, spanning 50 centers across the US, Canada, Europe, the Middle East, and Australia, core-lab-adjudicated implant depth in 603 transfemoral TAVR patients (Evolut PRO/PRO+) across four strata (<1mm, 1-3mm, 3-5mm, >5mm). New permanent pacemaker rates rose steeply with deeper implantation — 2.3%, 9.2%, 15.9%, and 20.3% respectively (P<.001) — providing the largest core-laboratory dataset yet showing that shallower implant depth meaningfully reduces post-TAVR pacemaker need.
MITRAL EuroIntervention · 2026-07-20
Temporal trends in mitral edge-to-edge repair for primary mitral regurgitation
The PRIME-MR registry analyzed 3,082 patients (median age 82, 45.1% women) undergoing mitral transcatheter edge-to-edge repair (M-TEER) for primary mitral regurgitation across 27 European and North American centers, comparing four procedural eras from 2009-2013 through 2020-2023. Complication rates fell from 42.4% to 23.1%, residual MR ≤1+ rose from 54.0% to 68.4%, and 1-year freedom from MR recurrence or deterioration improved from 63.6% to 74.4% — showing steady gains in procedural safety, efficacy, and durability as M-TEER technique and technology have matured.
TAVI EuroIntervention · 2026-07-24
Clinical and Haemodynamic Outcomes With Contemporary Intra- and Supra-Annular Self-Expanding Valves: The Multicentre International HERA-TAVI Registry
The multicentre international HERA-TAVI registry compared 2,607 TAVR patients treated with intra-annular (n=1,604) versus supra-annular (n=1,003) self-expanding valves; after propensity-score matching (892 pairs), 1-year VARC-3 device success was similar (91.1% vs 90.9%) and the composite primary endpoint did not differ (12.6% vs 11.3%), but permanent pacemaker implantation was higher with intra-annular valves (22.1% vs 16.3%) while major/life-threatening bleeding was higher with supra-annular valves (4.2% vs 2.6%) — supporting an individualized, anatomy- and lifetime-management-based approach to self-expanding valve selection.
TRICUSPID JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions · 2026-07-21
Incidence, Clinical Implications, and Predictors of Paravalvular Leak Following Transcatheter Tricuspid Valve Replacement: The TRIPLACE Registry
The multicentre TRIPLACE registry of 394 transcatheter tricuspid valve replacement (TTVR) patients found moderate-or-severe paravalvular leak (PVL) in 6.1% (n=24), mild PVL in 22.3% (n=88), and none/trace in 71.6% (n=282); moderate-or-severe PVL was independently associated with higher 1-year mortality and worse NYHA functional class. Larger right atrial volume, device malposition, and type IV annular morphology were the main predictors, informing patient selection and implantation technique.
MITRAL JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions · 2026-07-21
Clinical Outcomes of Transfemoral Transcatheter Mitral Valve Replacement: Results From the CHOICE-MI Registry
The international CHOICE-MI registry enrolled 124 patients (median age 79, 50% women, EuroSCORE II 5.0%) across 41 centers worldwide who underwent transfemoral transcatheter mitral valve replacement (TF-TMVR) with 9 different dedicated devices. Technical success was 91% with 0.8% procedural mortality; 95% of patients had residual mitral regurgitation ≤1+ at discharge, a benefit largely sustained through 2-year follow-up — pooled multi-device evidence for transfemoral TMVR in patients unsuitable for TEER or surgery.
TAVI Cardiovascular Business · 2026-07-22
Medical societies take a stand as CMS nears final decision on TAVR proposal
As the public comment period on CMS's proposed revision to the TAVR National Coverage Determination closed July 15, the ACC, SCAI, and STS sent a joint letter to CMS — signed by their presidents Roxana Mehran, J. Dawn Abbott, and Vinay Badhwar — urging preservation of the multidisciplinary Heart Team model, a targeted coverage-with-evidence-development pathway for populations with remaining evidence gaps, and quality-based rather than rigid volume-threshold operator requirements. CMS's proposal would drop CED for symptomatic patients and loosen the requirement for joint interventional cardiologist/surgeon involvement — changes that, if finalized, would reshape TAVR access and oversight nationwide.
TAVI Cardiovascular Business · 2026-07-23
Failure to rescue after TAVR: New study tracks outcomes at high- and low-volume hospitals
An STS/ACC TVT Registry analysis of 383,395 TAVR procedures across 808 US sites from 2017-2023 (median age 79) found major complications in 4,685 patients (1.2%), with in-hospital mortality of 26.0% among those with a major complication versus 1.0% among those without. The overall failure-to-rescue (FTR) rate was about 11%, declining slowly over the study period, and was similar at high- and low-volume hospitals — suggesting the well-known inverse volume-mortality relationship in TAVR is driven mainly by lower complication rates at high-volume centers rather than superior rescue capability once a complication occurs. Published in JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions (2026;19[13]:1735-1744; first author Tayyab Shah).
TAVI EuroIntervention · 2026-07-20
Early outcomes of redo-TAVI with the SAPIEN 3 platform: the prospective, multicentre ReTAVI registry
ReTAVI is the first prospective, international multicentre study designed specifically to evaluate 30-day safety and efficacy of redo-TAVI using the balloon-expandable SAPIEN 3 platform, enrolling 143 patients (median age 84, 40.6% women, median STS score 7.0%) across 59 centers in Europe, Canada, and Israel. Failed index valves were predominantly CoreValve/Evolut (53.1%), followed by SAPIEN 3 (30.1%) and ACURATE (14.0%) — providing the first systematic early data to guide reintervention strategy as the population of failed TAVI valves grows.
TAVI Federal Trade Commission · 2026-07-21
FTC Secures $12 Million in Penalties for Pre-Merger Reporting Act Violations
The FTC and DOJ announced on July 13, 2026 a record $12 million settlement — $10 million from Edwards Lifesciences/JC Medical and $2 million from Genesis MedTech — over Edwards' 2024 acquisition of aortic-regurgitation TAVR maker JC Medical, which was structured (a $115M deal price kept just under the $119.5M HSR threshold, paired with a separately-booked $25M investment in Genesis MedTech) to evade Hart-Scott-Rodino premerger notification — the largest-ever penalty for HSR filing avoidance. Edwards had moved to acquire JC Medical's only rival, JenaValve (Trilogy valve), the very next day, a deal a federal court preliminarily blocked in January 2026.
TRICUSPID MassDevice · 2026-07-22
Jenscare wins CE mark for transvenous tricuspid valve replacement
Chinese device maker Jenscare Scientific announced its second-generation transvenous tricuspid valve replacement system, LuX-Valve Plus, has received CE certification under EU MDR, making it the world's second approved transcatheter orthotopic TTVR system. The device uses a radial-force-independent septal anchoring design and a self-adaptive fabric skirt intended to reduce paravalvular leak in patients with severe tricuspid regurgitation.
