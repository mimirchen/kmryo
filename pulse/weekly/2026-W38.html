Prognostic Value of NYHA Functional Class in Tricuspid Valve Transcatheter Edge-to-Edge Repair: Insights From EuroTR
JACC: Advances  ·  2026-09-16
The European EuroTR registry analyzed 3,467 patients undergoing tricuspid transcatheter edge-to-edge repair (T-TEER), with 3,424 having baseline NYHA functional class data. Two-year all-cause mortality was 28.4% (619/3,424; 95% CI 26.4%-30.4%), and each one-class increase in baseline NYHA class was associated with an 80% higher 2-year mortality risk (HR 1.8, 95% CI 1.5-2.1, p<0.001). Among 1,974 patients with paired follow-up data, the proportion in NYHA class I/II rose from 16.4% at baseline to 59.4% at follow-up, underscoring baseline symptom severity as a strong independent predictor of outcomes and supporting earlier referral for intervention.

TAVI JACC: Advances · 2026-09-20
Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement for Severe Bicuspid Aortic Valve Stenosis: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis
A systematic review and meta-analysis in JACC: Advances pooled 52 studies (~295,615 patients) comparing TAVR outcomes in bicuspid (BAV) versus tricuspid (TAV) aortic stenosis, and TAVR versus surgical aortic valve replacement (SAVR) in BAV patients. Versus TAV, TAVR in BAV carried higher risks of paravalvular leak (RR 1.44), aortic root injury (RR 1.83), 30-day stroke (RR 1.27) and 30-day mortality (RR 1.25), but lower 1-year mortality (RR 0.84). Versus SAVR in BAV patients, TAVR had significantly higher permanent pacemaker rates (OR 2.29), shorter hospital stays, and less bleeding (OR 0.31), with no significant difference in mortality, acute kidney injury, stroke, or vascular complications — supporting TAVR as feasible in carefully selected BAV anatomy at experienced centers.
TAVI JACC: Asia · 2026-09-18
Ten-Year Clinical Outcomes and Valve Durability After Transcatheter Aortic Valve Implantation With Balloon-Expandable Valves
A Japanese multicenter registry analysis followed 297 patients who received early-generation SAPIEN XT balloon-expandable valves between 2010 and 2014 (mean age 83.8±6.0 years, STS score 7.5±4.0) for up to 10 years to assess long-term outcomes and valve durability. Freedom from all-cause mortality at 10 years was 13.2%, while cumulative bioprosthetic valve failure per VARC-3 criteria rose from 0.8% at 1 year to 3.6% at 5 years and 10.8% at 10 years, with severe structural valve deterioration in 3.0% and reintervention required in 4.5%. The findings suggest reassuring long-term durability for early-generation balloon-expandable valves, with 10-year mortality driven mainly by comorbidities rather than valve dysfunction.
MITRAL JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions · 2026-09-20
Predictors of Left Atrial Compliance and V-Wave Response in Patients Undergoing Transcatheter Mitral Valve Therapy
In 255 patients who underwent transcatheter mitral edge-to-edge repair or valve-in-valve replacement at Cleveland Clinic (2019-2023), 107 (42%) had a compliant left atrium (LA) and 148 (58%) a noncompliant LA. Intraprocedural LA v-wave pressure fell from 30.5±15.0 to 23.2±10.4 mmHg and mean LA pressure from 16.6±6.3 to 15.3±5.9 mmHg after the procedure. On multivariable analysis, low LA reservoir strain and prior AF intervention (ablation or LAA ligation) independently predicted low LA compliance, and a residual post-procedural v-wave above 25 mmHg was linked to markedly worse 3-year mortality (46.8% vs. 34.2% for v-wave ≤25 mmHg).
TAVI Structural Heart · 2026-09-15
Sex-Specific Differences in Clinical Outcomes After Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement: A Meta-Analysis of Reconstructed Individual Patient Survival Data
A reconstructed individual-patient-data meta-analysis of 71 studies and 481,353 TAVR patients, published in the September 2026 issue of Structural Heart, found women had higher 30-day mortality (HR 1.064, 95% CI 1.005-1.127, p=0.033) and stroke/TIA risk (HR 1.511, 95% CI 1.317-1.734, p<0.001) than men, along with more life-threatening bleeding (RR 1.29), major bleeding (RR 1.26) and major vascular complications (RR 1.66). Despite these early hazards, women showed a sustained long-term survival advantage across valve generations — a pattern an accompanying editorial calls the "female paradox" in TAVR, urging dedicated strategies to curb women's periprocedural bleeding and vascular risk.
OTHER Structural Heart · 2026-09-17
Patterns and Timing of Sequential Transcatheter Valve Interventions After Index TAVR, M-TEER, and TMVR: A Nationwide Medicare Analysis
A nationwide US Medicare fee-for-service cohort study (2017-2023) of 527,976 patients undergoing index transcatheter valve interventions (461,272 TAVR, 59,782 mitral TEER, 6,922 TMVR) found that additional transcatheter valve interventions remained uncommon, with 5-year cumulative incidences of 0.86% after TAVR, 2.69% after M-TEER, and 4.48% after TMVR. The dominant sequences were TAVR→M-TEER, M-TEER→TAVR, and TMVR→TAVR, with median times to the second procedure of 229, 300, and 483 days respectively, offering benchmark data for heart-team planning of long-term surveillance and sequential valve therapy.
TAVI Structural Heart · 2026-09-18
Impact of Early Nonfatal Complications After Transcatheter Aortic Valve Implantation on 1-Year Mortality and Quality of Life: Insights From the LANDMARK Trial
A post-hoc substudy of the randomized LANDMARK trial (Myval vs. contemporary Sapien/Evolut valves) analyzed 742 patients alive at 30 days after TAVI using the VARC-3 early safety endpoint (stroke, type 3 bleeding, major vascular complication, AKI stage 2-4, moderate/severe paravalvular leak, new pacemaker, or reintervention). Of these, 177 (23.9%) experienced at least one early nonfatal complication, which was independently associated with significantly higher mortality between 30 and 365 days, although 1-year quality of life was similar among survivors regardless of complication status — supporting calls to redefine TAVI procedural success beyond 30-day mortality alone.
TRICUSPID EuroIntervention · 2026-09-16
Outcomes of patients with large anatomies undergoing transcatheter tricuspid valve replacement with the LuX-Valve Plus system
EuroIntervention published data from the European TRINITY cohort enrolling 114 patients with severe tricuspid regurgitation and annuli too large for standard devices (fitted with 55-70 mm valves), who underwent transjugular transcatheter tricuspid valve replacement with the LuX-Valve Plus system (Jenscare). Mean age was 77.8±5.8 years with a high STS risk score of 9.9%±6.0%. Intraprocedural success was 95.6% with no intraprocedural deaths or strokes, and 30-day clinical success was 91.2%, with residual severe TR the leading cause of failure — extending TTVR to high-risk patients previously excluded for oversized anatomy.
TAVI EuroIntervention · 2026-09-17
A randomized trial of carbon-dioxide flushing to reduce vascular brain injury in patients undergoing TAVI (INTERCEPTavi)
The INTERCEPTavi trial, a single-centre, blinded randomized pilot study, randomized 60 patients undergoing TAVI 1:1 to valve flushing with carbon dioxide plus saline versus saline alone, with new cerebral lesions on postprocedural MRI as the primary endpoint. CO2 flushing significantly reduced the mean number of new lesions per patient (4 vs 8.5; p=0.031), with a numerically smaller total infarct area (40.32 mm² vs 93.20 mm²) that did not reach significance (p=0.111) — a low-cost, easily adoptable technique that may offer additional neuroprotection during TAVI pending larger confirmatory trials.
TAVI EuroIntervention · 2026-09-18
Routine post-dilatation at nominal volume to optimise the expansion of balloon-expandable valves: the DOUBLE-TAP study
The DOUBLE-TAP study evaluated routine post-dilatation at the same nominal balloon volume (a 'double-tap' technique, avoiding additional oversizing) after balloon-expandable TAVI. Before the second inflation, 49.6% of patients had prosthesis expansion below 80%; afterward, underexpansion persisted in only 0.9% of patients. The deformation index improved (1.05 to 1.03; p<0.001), median transvalvular gradient fell by 6.5 mmHg (p=0.036), and paravalvular leak was reduced to trivial or none in all patients. Safety was preserved, with 30-day mortality of 0.9%, stroke 3.5%, and new pacemaker implantation 6.2%; bicuspid anatomy and valvular calcium volume >385 mm³ independently predicted greater improvement. The findings suggest this simple, low-cost technique can safely optimise valve expansion in favourable anatomies.
MITRAL JACC: Advances · 2026-09-15
Sex-Based Differences in Outcomes After Transcatheter Edge-to-Edge Repair for Severe Mitral Regurgitation
A single-center study of 293 patients undergoing mitral transcatheter edge-to-edge repair (M-TEER) for severe mitral regurgitation, published ahead-of-print in JACC: Advances (2026), found women (119 patients, 40.6%) had a lower burden of coronary artery disease than men (65% vs 81%, p=0.002) and less functional MR (37% vs 47%), while 6-month all-cause mortality did not differ significantly by sex (17% in women vs 22% in men, p=0.301) — suggesting broadly comparable short-term survival benefit of M-TEER despite differing baseline cardiovascular risk profiles between sexes.
TRICUSPID JACC: Asia · 2026-09-18
Transcatheter Edge-to-Edge Repair for Tricuspid Regurgitation: Design and Rationale of the TEER-CHINA Trial
TEER-CHINA is the first prospective, multicenter randomized controlled trial of a China-developed dedicated tricuspid transcatheter edge-to-edge repair (TEER) platform, the DragonFly-T system. The trial will enroll 236 patients with severe or greater (≥3+) tricuspid regurgitation who remain symptomatic despite at least 30 days of optimal medical therapy (OMT), are at intermediate or higher surgical risk, and have suitable anatomy, across 35 centers in China. Patients will be randomized 1:1 to DragonFly-T plus OMT versus OMT alone, with a 12-month hierarchical composite primary endpoint of all-cause mortality or tricuspid valve intervention, annualized heart failure hospitalization, and Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire health status improvement. As the first randomized evaluation of a China-developed dedicated tricuspid TEER platform, the trial aims to establish local evidence amid rapid growth of tricuspid interventions in Asia.
