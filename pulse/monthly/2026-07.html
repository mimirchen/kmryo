Procedural Strategies for Optimal Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement: An International Position Statement
JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions  ·  2026-07-13
A Rigshospitalet-led international position statement from 30+ experts proposes the TAVR CODE framework (Coaxiality, Orientation, Depth, Expansion) of four fluoroscopic parameters to standardize intraprocedural TAVR assessment, and details optimization for younger, longer-life-expectancy patients — pre/post-dilatation, commissural alignment, IVUS expansion checks and AI-robotic delivery.

LAA EuroIntervention · 2026-07-06
Left atrial appendage closure with the AnchorMan or Watchman devices: 12-month results of the SAFE-PROTECT randomised trial
SAFE-PROTECT, a Chinese multicentre open-label non-inferiority RCT, randomized 216 high-stroke-risk NVAF patients to the new AnchorMan vs Watchman 2.5 for LAA closure: 12-month clinical success 98.1% in both (non-inferiority P=0.0004), 100% echo closure, and fewer >3 mm peridevice leaks with AnchorMan (5.7% vs 15.6%, P=0.04).
OTHER JAMA Cardiology · 2026-07-21
Twenty-Year Follow-Up After Patent Foramen Ovale Closure in Patients With Paradoxical Embolism
This single-center cohort followed 130 patients who underwent transcatheter PFO closure between 2001 and 2006 for a PFO-associated stroke (n=91), TIA (n=45), or peripheral embolism (n=1) for up to 20 years — among the longest follow-up periods reported for PFO closure. Twenty patients (15.3%) died (12.2% from non-cardiovascular causes), while recurrent ischemic events were rare: just 1 stroke (0.04 per 100 patient-years) and 6 TIAs (0.25 per 100 patient-years), confirming durable very-long-term safety and efficacy of PFO closure for paradoxical embolism.
TAVI JAMA Cardiology · 2026-07-22
Seven-Year Valve Durability With Transcatheter or Surgical Aortic Valve Replacement: An Ad Hoc Analysis of the PARTNER 3 Randomized Clinical Trial
An ad hoc 7-year durability analysis of the low-risk PARTNER 3 RCT (1,000 patients — 495 TAVR, 453 SAVR — across 71 US/Canada centers, last follow-up March 2025) found comparable bioprosthetic valve failure (6.9% TAVR vs 7.3% SAVR) and aortic valve reintervention rates (6.0% vs 6.7%) between arms. Thrombosis-related valve dysfunction was more common with TAVR (5.2% vs 0.9%) but was mostly early-onset, reversible, and rarely progressed to failure — supporting comparable long-term durability of the balloon-expandable SAPIEN 3 valve versus surgery for lifetime management in low-risk patients.
TAVI EHJ · 2026-07-01
Physiology vs angiography-guided PCI in TAVI: the FAITAVI trial
Italian multicentre RCT comparing FFR (physiology)- vs angiography-guided PCI in TAVI patients with coronary disease, giving randomized evidence on how to guide revascularization in this contested area; published with an accompanying editorial.
TAVI Circulation · 2026-07-01
Contemporary Outcomes of Aortic Stenosis Care: The AHA Target: Aortic Stenosis Registry
The AHA Target: Aortic Stenosis quality registry maps real-world diagnosis-to-treatment pathways and outcomes for severe aortic stenosis in the US, exposing treatment delays and referral gaps — a baseline for closing the aortic-stenosis 'care gap'.
TAVI EHJ Open · 2026-07-30
Cost-effectiveness analysis of transcatheter aortic valve implantation for asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis across nine European countries
Using a lifetime-horizon health-economic model built on EARLY TAVR trial inputs (901 patients with asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis randomized to early TAVI vs clinical surveillance, median follow-up 3.8 years), researchers assessed early TAVI's cost-effectiveness across nine European health systems (Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, UK). Early TAVI was economically dominant in all nine countries, with cost-effectiveness probability ranging from 97.32% (UK) to 99.94% (Belgium), lifetime savings of £1,788 (UK) to CHF15,802 (Switzerland) per patient, and QALY gains of 0.18 (Germany/UK) to 0.23 (Switzerland); early TAVI also cut disabling stroke by 0.04-0.05, non-disabling stroke by 0.03, and heart-failure hospitalization by 0.12-0.14 events per person. Published in European Heart Journal Open, the analysis lands as CMS weighs expanding US TAVR coverage to asymptomatic patients.
TAVI New York Valves 2026 · 2026-07-01
TAVI holds up to SAVR at seven years on durability in PARTNER 3
At New York Valves 2026 (and in JAMA Cardiology), the PARTNER 3 7-year analysis found SAPIEN 3 TAVI and surgery had similar structural valve deterioration (7.3% vs 7.6%), bioprosthetic failure (6.9% vs 7.5%) and reintervention (5.4% vs 5.5%) in low-risk patients; TAVI had more thrombosis-related dysfunction (5.2% vs 0.9%) but mostly early and often reversible — supporting TAVI durability for lifetime management.
MITRAL JACC: Cardiovascular Imaging · 2026-07-08
Sex-Related Differences in Outcomes and Thresholds for Intervention in Primary Mitral Regurgitation: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis
A meta-analysis of 17 studies and 25,690 patients undergoing surgical or transcatheter repair for primary MR (37.5% women): women were treated older (by 3.2 years) and more symptomatic, with 15% higher long-term all-cause mortality (HR 1.15, 95% CI 1.07–1.24) and 37% higher MACE — suggesting LV-centric thresholds systematically delay women and should be revisited.
TAVI JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions · 2026-07-13
Hospital Volume and Failure to Rescue Post-TAVR: Insights From the STS/ACC TVT Registry
A 487,159-patient STS/ACC TVT registry cohort (808 sites, 2017–2023): low-volume centers had higher 30-day mortality (adj OR 1.12) and major complications (adj OR 1.19) than high-volume, but failure-to-rescue (11.0% overall) did not differ by volume — implying the volume-mortality link is driven by complication rates, not rescue ability.
TAVI JACC: Cardiovascular Interventions · 2026-07-21
First Large Comprehensive Core-Laboratory Evaluation of Implantation Depth and Clinical Outcomes in TAVR: Final Global Results From the Optimize PRO Prospective Study
The prospective Optimize PRO study, spanning 50 centers across the US, Canada, Europe, the Middle East, and Australia, core-lab-adjudicated implant depth in 603 transfemoral TAVR patients (Evolut PRO/PRO+) across four strata (<1mm, 1-3mm, 3-5mm, >5mm). New permanent pacemaker rates rose steeply with deeper implantation — 2.3%, 9.2%, 15.9%, and 20.3% respectively (P<.001) — providing the largest core-laboratory dataset yet showing that shallower implant depth meaningfully reduces post-TAVR pacemaker need.
TRICUSPID MassDevice · 2026-07-08
Jenscare wins CE mark (EU MDR) for LuX-Valve Plus transvenous tricuspid valve replacement system
Jenscare's second-generation transvenous tricuspid valve replacement system, LuX-Valve Plus, won EU MDR CE mark — the world's second approved orthotopic transcatheter tricuspid replacement; its septal-anchoring design (not reliant on radial force) may lower pacemaker risk, with 1,000+ LuX-Valve implants across dozens of countries.
TAVI EuroIntervention · 2026-07-06
Prosthesis-patient mismatch with intra-annular self-expanding valves: an analysis of the PORTICO IDE trial
A core-lab analysis of the PORTICO IDE RCT (675 high/extreme-risk severe AS) comparing Portico, Evolut and SAPIEN 3 by annulus size: in small annuli the intra-annular self-expanding Portico beat balloon-expandable SAPIEN 3 (mean gradient 8.5 vs 13.7 mmHg, severe PPM 3.3% vs 26.3%, both P<0.0001) with lower 5-year deterioration (2.2% vs 13.9%) — challenging the self-expanding-vs-balloon dichotomy.
MITRAL EuroIntervention · 2026-07-20
Temporal trends in mitral edge-to-edge repair for primary mitral regurgitation
The PRIME-MR registry analyzed 3,082 patients (median age 82, 45.1% women) undergoing mitral transcatheter edge-to-edge repair (M-TEER) for primary mitral regurgitation across 27 European and North American centers, comparing four procedural eras from 2009-2013 through 2020-2023. Complication rates fell from 42.4% to 23.1%, residual MR ≤1+ rose from 54.0% to 68.4%, and 1-year freedom from MR recurrence or deterioration improved from 63.6% to 74.4% — showing steady gains in procedural safety, efficacy, and durability as M-TEER technique and technology have matured.
TAVI EuroIntervention · 2026-07-24
Clinical and Haemodynamic Outcomes With Contemporary Intra- and Supra-Annular Self-Expanding Valves: The Multicentre International HERA-TAVI Registry
The multicentre international HERA-TAVI registry compared 2,607 TAVR patients treated with intra-annular (n=1,604) versus supra-annular (n=1,003) self-expanding valves; after propensity-score matching (892 pairs), 1-year VARC-3 device success was similar (91.1% vs 90.9%) and the composite primary endpoint did not differ (12.6% vs 11.3%), but permanent pacemaker implantation was higher with intra-annular valves (22.1% vs 16.3%) while major/life-threatening bleeding was higher with supra-annular valves (4.2% vs 2.6%) — supporting an individualized, anatomy- and lifetime-management-based approach to self-expanding valve selection.
